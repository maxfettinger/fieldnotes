In late August we walked a part of the Transcaucasian Trail through Upper Svaneti, in the Caucasus of north-western
Georgia: eleven days, one of them spent resting, 138 km and about 8,500 m of climbing. The highest pass was at about
3,400 m.

A trip post is a page bundle: this `index.md`, the route as `route.geojson` and a folder of photos per gallery.

{{< trip-map geojson="route.geojson" caption="Ten days of walking, ten nights" >}}

The map draws each day in the colour its GeoJSON feature names (`stroke`), here every other day in blue, or else in
the colour scheme's accent, and the nights as points (`marker-color`). Click a day for its distance and climbing.

## The lakes

The photos are the folder `lakes/`, in the order of their names; `photos.yaml` next to it has their alt text,
captions and places. The last one is a video: `06-drift.mp4` with `06-drift.jpg` as its poster.
