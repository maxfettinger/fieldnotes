A trip is a post with files: the route as a GeoJSON file, and folders of photos. Fieldnotes draws the route on a map
and turns each folder into a row of photos that opens in a full-screen viewer.

- [A trip, start to finish](a-trip-start-to-finish/): the steps, with a ready-made template.
- [Photo galleries](photo-galleries/): folders, captions, videos, and keeping your location private.
- [Trip maps](trip-maps/): the route file, its colours, and the free MapTiler key the map needs.

The demo's [Hut to Hut in the Stubai Alps](https://fieldnotes.maxfettinger.com/trips/alps/) has a map and two
galleries.
