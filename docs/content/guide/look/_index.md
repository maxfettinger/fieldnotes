The look has two parts you choose independently: a **preset**, how the site is drawn (fonts, background, cards,
decoration), and a **colour scheme**, its colours in a light and a dark version. Any scheme goes with any preset.
On top of both, you can change the fonts, the background and single colours.

The quickest way to choose is the [demo's home page](https://fieldnotes.maxfettinger.com/): its card switches presets
and schemes as you click, and shows the settings to copy. The [theme
generator](https://fieldnotes.maxfettinger.com/generator/) adds fonts and backgrounds.
